## Application authorization

Reviewed `access.py`, `auth.py`, `deps.py`, scenario/simulation/comparison/report routes and reuse. `visible`/`can_read` enforce public-or-owner; job subresources and reports use those checks. Role claims do not grant an administrative bypass. JWT validation requires a supported signature algorithm, audience, expiry, and UUID subject; invalid present tokens are rejected rather than downgraded to anonymous. `can_modify` intentionally permits modification of ownerless demo jobs; that is a documented demo policy, not owner isolation for authenticated records.

New `services/api/tests/test_authz_owner_isolation.py` covers:

1. Private job detail/metrics/result/artifacts/JSON and Markdown reports and cancel are hidden from Bob and anon, with owner-positive controls.
2. Status/network/hash/mine filters cannot widen list visibility.
3. Comparison creation checks both source jobs; private comparison/report endpoints remain owner-only.
4. Signed service/admin role claims do not escalate, missing-subject tokens and unsigned tokens are rejected.
5. Reuse cannot expose an unreadable private source, while the owner can reuse their private result.
6. Reuse of a public source preserves the new caller's ownership/private default; public-read access does not authorize cancelling another owner's job.

Before the service fix, `uv run pytest -q tests/test_authz_owner_isolation.py` produced **1 failed, 4 passed, 1 warning in 4.06s**: `test_result_reuse_does_not_leak_private_runs` compared identical forbidden source IDs. The temporary HTTP probe also showed Bob reusing Alice's private job and anon receiving an unreadable reuse source. Initial ruff format/check succeeded. The tests were subsequently strengthened to six tests; **that final version has not been rerun here**. Storage owns the service fix, and Main owns integrated pass-after verification. No xfail, weakened assertion, or authorization shim was added.

Source review: worker polls process-tree RSS approximately every 0.25 s, kills the tree at the configured threshold, records `failed/memory_limit`; deadline records `timeout/timeout`. Queued cancellation becomes cancelled immediately; running cancellation kills the local subprocess, or is noticed via the database heartbeat (default 5 s). POSIX workers use separate process groups; BLAS/OpenMP are pinned to one thread, and Linux child oom_score_adj prefers the engine over the API. The RSS budget is a polling watchdog, **not a hard container-wide 400 MB reservation**; transient overshoot/API overhead remain possible on the 512 MB host. Unattributed SIGKILL is classified as memory_limit in source, but SIGKILL alone is not independent proof of kernel OOM.

No new destructive production timeout/cancel/memory test was run. Existing tests to include in Main's integrated gate: `test_jobs.py::test_timeout`, `test_memory_watchdog_kills_engine`, `test_cancel_running_and_queued`, and `test_engine_oom.py` (Linux-only assertion requires Linux). This audit does not relabel those tests as newly passed.
